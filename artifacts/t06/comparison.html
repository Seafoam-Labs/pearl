Pearl desktop beside the DMS reference

These are actual, unedited application captures. DMS is the frozen T00 reference; Pearl is the live T06 shell in a private Aqueous session with real desktop-file entries and independent GTK windows. Search, workspace/window activation, keyboard state and layout controls are implemented. Services assigned to later tasks are explicitly unavailable.

The shared treatment is a rounded 48-pixel bar, purple selection, tonal panels, compact icon controls and grouped content. Pearl's 620×600 launcher centers in usable output bounds and includes a heading/result footer. Its 600-pixel control center is taller because native Aqueous layout controls are present. Calendar and control-center panes follow the occupied bar edge. The comparison is a design review, not a claim of pixel identity.
